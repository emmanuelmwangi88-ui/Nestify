1What Nestify is

Nestify is a marketplace where independent agents and agencies advertise rentals and short stays in Kenya. Nestify is not the landlord, host, hotel operator or agent, and is not a party to any tenancy or booking you agree with an agent.

2Check before you pay

  • Visit the property, or send someone you trust, before you pay anything.
  • Confirm the agent’s identity and authority to let the property.
  • Pay only to the account named in a written agreement.
  • Never pay for a place you have not seen, and never share your M-Pesa PIN with anyone.

3Listings and agents

Agents are responsible for prices, photos, availability and every claim they make. Nestify may label, suspend or remove a listing that breaks its rules, but does not inspect every property and does not guarantee any listing.

4Enquiries and booking requests

An enquiry or booking request is a message, not a confirmed booking. A booking exists only when the agent confirms it to you. After you send a request the agent may contact you by WhatsApp, email or phone, and the conversation continues outside Nestify.

5Reviews

Anyone may review a live listing. Reviews must be honest, about the property, and free of personal data and abuse. Nestify may hide reviews that break these rules, such as spam, abuse, personal data or fake reviews, but does not hide a review only because it is critical.

6Respect others

Do not use Nestify for fraud, impersonation, harassment, scraping or unlawful content. We may suspend access to protect other people or to comply with the law.

7When you send an enquiry or booking request

We collect your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, message, preferred way to be contacted, and your consent. We pass these to the agent of that listing so they can reply to you. After that, the agent talks to you outside Nestify and looks after your details for that conversation.

8When you write a review

We collect your name, email and review text. Your email is never shown and is stored only as a one-way hash to stop duplicate reviews. A hashed form of your network address is used for abuse protection, and the original is not kept.

9Agents and payments

Agents give their name, phone, email, agency and licence details. Payments are handled by M-Pesa (Safaricom). We store the receipt number, amount, phone number and status of each payment, and never your M-Pesa PIN.

10Your choices and rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or to stop an agent contacting you. Email the support address shown in the site footer and tell us which enquiry or review you mean.

11Where data is kept

Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase. Your browser stores a few things on your own device, such as saved properties and your sign-in session. We do not run advertising trackers.

12Agents

Agents who list on Nestify also accept the Agent agreement. It covers listing rules, subscriptions, customer contact and reviews.
