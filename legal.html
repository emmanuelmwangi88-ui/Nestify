Terms of use

Nestify is a marketplace where independent agents and agencies advertise rentals and short stays in Kenya. Nestify is not the landlord, host, hotel operator or agent, and is not a party to any tenancy or booking you agree with an agent.

  • Check before you pay. Visit the property or send someone you trust. Confirm the agent’s identity and authority, and pay only to the account named in a written agreement. Never pay for a place you have not seen.
  • Listings come from agents. Agents are responsible for prices, photos, availability and every claim they make. Nestify may label, suspend or remove a listing that breaks its rules.
  • Enquiries and booking requests are messages, not confirmed bookings. A booking exists only when the agent confirms it to you.
  • Reviews. Anyone may review a live listing. Reviews must be honest, about the property, and free of personal data and abuse. Nestify may hide reviews that break these rules.
  • Respect others. No fraud, impersonation, harassment, scraping or unlawful content.

Privacy notice

This explains what we collect and why, in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

  • When you send an enquiry or booking request we collect your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, message, preferred way to be contacted, and your consent. We pass these to the agent of that listing so they can reply to you on WhatsApp, email or phone. After that, the agent talks to you outside Nestify and handles your details for that conversation.
  • When you write a review we collect your name, email and review. Your email is never shown and is stored only as a one-way hash to stop duplicates. A hashed form of your network address is used for abuse protection.
  • Agents give their name, phone, email, agency and licence number, and payment details are handled by M-Pesa (Safaricom). We store the receipt number, amount and status, never your M-Pesa PIN.
  • Your choices. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or to stop an agent contacting you, by emailing the support address in the page footer.
  • Storage. Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase. Browser storage is used only to remember things like your saved properties on your own device.
