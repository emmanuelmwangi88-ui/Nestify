Before you pay anyone

Nestify shows who listed each property and when it was last updated. It cannot check a property for you, so a few habits protect your money.

  • View it firstVisit in person or send someone you trust. Never pay for a place you have not seen.
  • Confirm the agentAsk for ID and proof that they can let or manage the property.
  • Match the payment detailsPay only to the account named in the written agreement.
  • Report anything oddPressure to pay quickly or a price that looks too good? Use "Report this listing" on the property.
