0Parties

This Agent Platform Agreement (“Agreement”) is between Nestify Ltd (“Nestify”, “we”, “us”) and the person or entity accepting it (“Agent”, “you”). By accepting electronically you confirm that you have authority to bind the named agency and agree to the following.

1Platform service

Nestify provides tools to publish and manage premium property, hotel, resort and short-stay listings; receive enquiries and booking requests; display maps and media; and show open customer reviews.

Nestify is a marketplace technology provider unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. Nestify is not the owner, landlord, hotel operator or booking principal for an Agent’s listing.

2Agent authority and identity

You confirm that:

  • your registration and contact details are complete and accurate;
  • you are legally authorised by the property owner or operator to advertise every listing you publish;
  • you will provide evidence of authority, identity or property rights when reasonably requested;
  • you will not impersonate another person, agency, hotel or property owner.

3Listing accuracy

You are responsible for every statement, price, fee, tax, availability claim, location, amenity, policy, image and video you submit. Media must be current, lawfully obtained and representative of the actual property.

You must promptly correct or archive inaccurate or unavailable listings. You may not publish duplicates, bait pricing, misleading locations, unavailable properties or manipulated media.

4Luxury and hospitality standards

Listings must fall within Nestify’s accepted premium categories: luxury homes, villas, penthouses, serviced apartments, boutique hotels, resorts, lodges or professionally managed BnBs. Nestify may set category-specific completeness and quality requirements, including minimum media, amenity, location and description standards.

5Subscription and M-Pesa payment

Publishing requires an active paid subscription. Nestify offers three plans, Starter, Growth and Elite, each with a monthly price and a limit on active listings.

The current price and listing limit of each plan are shown on the Subscription page of your workspace. The price you pay is the price shown when you start the payment. Nestify may change plan prices for future payments and will not change a payment already made.
  • Payment is made through M-Pesa and takes effect only after Safaricom confirms it.
  • Fees are charged monthly. They are non-refundable once a listing has been published, except where the law requires otherwise or Nestify expressly approves a refund.
  • Failed, reversed or disputed payments may suspend publication.
  • Taxes, if applicable, are shown or handled according to law.

6Automatic publication and audits

A complete listing may publish automatically while your subscription is active. Automatic publication is not an endorsement or guarantee by Nestify.

Nestify may audit, label, restrict, suspend or remove any listing or account after publication where there is suspected fraud, inaccuracy, illegality, safety risk, rights infringement, poor quality, non-payment or breach of this Agreement.

7Enquiries, bookings and talking to customers

When a customer sends an enquiry or booking request they give their name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address and preferred way to be contacted, and they agree that you may contact them.

  • You may reply by WhatsApp, email or phone, and continue the conversation outside Nestify.
  • You may use those details only to answer that enquiry or booking.
  • You are responsible for confirming availability, price and terms directly with the customer.
  • You must stop contacting a customer who asks you to stop.

8Customer reviews

Anyone may review a live listing without an invitation. Because of this, you must not buy, fabricate, suppress, threaten or pressure anyone about reviews, or review your own listings.

  • You may reply to a review professionally and factually.
  • You cannot edit or delete reviews.
  • Nestify may hide reviews that break its rules, for example abuse, personal data, spam or fake reviews, but will not hide a review only because it is critical.

9Content licence

You retain ownership of content you lawfully own. You grant Nestify a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence during the listing period to host, resize, display, distribute and promote submitted content for operating and marketing the platform. You confirm that doing so does not violate another party’s rights.

10Data protection

Each party will handle personal data lawfully and securely. You may use customer details only to answer the relevant enquiry, manage the requested booking or tenancy, meet legal obligations, and provide the agreed service. You may not sell or misuse customer data. Nestify’s privacy notice explains its separate processing activities.

11Prohibited conduct

You may not use Nestify for fraud, discrimination prohibited by law, unlawful accommodation, harassment, malware, scraping, payment evasion, review manipulation, or content that infringes privacy, intellectual property or other rights.

12Suspension and termination

Either party may stop using the platform. Nestify may immediately suspend content or access where necessary to protect customers, comply with law, investigate misconduct or address non-payment.

Provisions intended to survive, including payment obligations, licences already exercised, liability, disputes and data duties, remain effective.

13Disclaimers and liability

To the extent permitted by law, the platform is provided on an “as available” basis. Nestify does not guarantee occupancy, enquiries, bookings, revenue, customer conduct or uninterrupted availability.

Each party remains responsible for its own fraud, negligence, legal compliance and contractual promises. Final liability limits and exclusions must be confirmed by Kenyan legal counsel.

14Indemnity

To the extent permitted by law, the Agent will defend and indemnify Nestify against third-party claims arising from the Agent’s listings, lack of authority, customer transactions, unlawful content, breached promises, taxes or violation of law, except to the extent caused by Nestify’s own wrongful conduct.

15Governing law and disputes

This Agreement is intended to be governed by the laws of Kenya. The parties should first attempt good-faith resolution. The final forum, mediation or arbitration process, notices and jurisdiction language must be confirmed by Kenyan legal counsel before launch.

16Electronic acceptance

You agree that clicking the acceptance checkboxes and submitting payment is electronic acceptance. Nestify may keep the agreement version, your legal name, account ID, timestamp, user agent and permitted technical metadata as evidence. You can download or request a copy.

17Changes

Nestify may update this Agreement going forward. Material changes will require renewed acceptance before you continue publishing on a paid plan.

What we record when you accept

  • Your agent account ID, legal name and agency
  • The agreement version and the terms you accepted (platform, content rules, payment)
  • The time of acceptance and your browser details
  • Your IP address, only where the server lawfully collects it
