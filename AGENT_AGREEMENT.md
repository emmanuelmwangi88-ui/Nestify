# Nestify Agent Platform Agreement
**Version 2026-10-15 — Draft for legal review**

> This is a product template, not legal advice. Before production use, Nestify’s owner should have a qualified Kenyan advocate review this agreement, the payment model, privacy notice, consumer-protection obligations, tax treatment and dispute clauses.

This Agent Platform Agreement (“Agreement”) is between **Nestify Ltd** (“Nestify”, “we”, “us”) and the person or entity accepting it (“Agent”, “you”). By accepting electronically, you confirm that you have authority to bind the named agency and agree to the following.

## 1. Platform service
Nestify provides tools to publish and manage premium property, hotel, resort and short-stay listings; receive enquiries and booking requests; display maps and media; and show open customer reviews. Nestify is a marketplace technology provider unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. Nestify is not the owner, landlord, hotel operator or booking principal for an Agent’s listing.

## 2. Agent authority and identity
You confirm that:
- your registration and contact details are complete and accurate;
- you are legally authorised by the property owner or operator to advertise every listing you publish;
- you will provide evidence of authority, identity or property rights when reasonably requested;
- you will not impersonate another person, agency, hotel or property owner.

## 3. Listing accuracy
You are responsible for every statement, price, fee, tax, availability claim, location, amenity, policy, image and video you submit. Media must be current, lawfully obtained and representative of the actual property. You must promptly correct or archive inaccurate or unavailable listings. You may not publish duplicates, bait pricing, misleading locations, unavailable properties or manipulated media that materially misrepresents the premises.

## 4. Luxury and hospitality standards
Listings must fall within Nestify’s accepted premium categories: luxury homes, villas, penthouses, serviced apartments, boutique hotels, resorts, lodges or professionally managed BnBs. Nestify may set category-specific completeness and quality requirements, including minimum media, amenity, location and description standards.

## 5. Subscription and M-Pesa payment
Publishing requires an active paid subscription:
- **Starter:** KSh 2,500 per month, up to 3 active listings;
- **Growth:** KSh 6,500 per month, up to 12 active listings;
- **Elite:** KSh 15,000 per month, up to 40 active listings.

Payment is initiated through M-Pesa and becomes effective only after confirmed provider callback. Fees are charged monthly and are non-refundable once a listing has been published, except where required by law or expressly approved by Nestify. Failed, reversed or disputed payments may suspend publication. Taxes, if applicable, will be shown or handled according to law.

## 6. Automatic publication and audits
A complete listing may publish automatically while your subscription is active. Automatic publication is not an endorsement or guarantee by Nestify. Nestify may audit, label, restrict, suspend or remove any listing or account after publication where there is suspected fraud, inaccuracy, illegality, safety risk, rights infringement, poor quality, non-payment or breach of this Agreement.

## 7. Enquiries, bookings and talking to customers
When a customer sends an enquiry or booking request they give their name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address and preferred way to be contacted, and they agree that you may contact them. You may reply by WhatsApp, email or phone, and continue the conversation outside Nestify. You may use those details only to answer that enquiry or booking. You are responsible for confirming availability, disclosing all charges, issuing lawful documents and fulfilling accepted stays or tenancies. A booking request is not a confirmed booking unless you confirm it. You may not request deceptive, unsafe or unlawful payment methods, and you must stop contacting a customer who asks you to stop. Nestify is not responsible for an Agent’s separate transaction with a customer.

## 8. Customer reviews
Anyone may review a live listing without an invitation. Because of this, you must not buy, fabricate, suppress, threaten or pressure anyone about reviews, or review your own listings. You may reply to a review professionally and factually. You cannot edit or delete reviews. Nestify may hide reviews that break its rules (for example abuse, personal data, spam or fake reviews) but will not hide genuine negative feedback merely because it is unfavourable.

## 9. Content licence
You retain ownership of content you lawfully own. You grant Nestify a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence during the listing period to host, resize, display, distribute and promote submitted content for operating and marketing the platform. You confirm that doing so does not violate another party’s rights.

## 10. Data protection
Each party will handle personal data lawfully and securely. You may use customer details only to answer the relevant enquiry, manage the requested booking or tenancy, meet legal obligations, and provide agreed service. You may not sell or misuse customer data. Nestify’s privacy notice explains its separate processing activities.

## 11. Prohibited conduct
You may not use Nestify for fraud, discrimination prohibited by law, unlawful accommodation, harassment, malware, scraping, payment evasion, review manipulation, or content that infringes privacy, intellectual property or other rights.

## 12. Suspension and termination
Either party may stop using the platform. Nestify may immediately suspend content or access where necessary to protect customers, comply with law, investigate misconduct or address non-payment. Provisions intended to survive—including payment obligations, licences already exercised, liability, disputes and data duties—remain effective.

## 13. Disclaimers and liability
To the extent permitted by law, the platform is provided on an “as available” basis. Nestify does not guarantee occupancy, enquiries, bookings, revenue, customer conduct or uninterrupted availability. Each party remains responsible for its own fraud, negligence, legal compliance and contractual promises. Final liability limits and exclusions must be confirmed by Kenyan legal counsel.

## 14. Indemnity
To the extent permitted by law, the Agent will defend and indemnify Nestify against third-party claims arising from the Agent’s listings, lack of authority, customer transactions, unlawful content, breached promises, taxes or violation of law, except to the extent caused by Nestify’s own wrongful conduct.

## 15. Governing law and disputes
This Agreement is intended to be governed by the laws of Kenya. The parties should first attempt good-faith resolution. The final forum, mediation/arbitration process, notices and jurisdiction language must be confirmed by Kenyan legal counsel before launch.

## 16. Electronic acceptance
You agree that clicking acceptance checkboxes and submitting payment constitutes electronic acceptance. Nestify may retain the agreement version, your legal name, account ID, timestamp, user agent and permitted technical metadata as evidence. You can download or request a copy.

## 17. Changes
Nestify may update this Agreement prospectively. Material changes will require renewed acceptance before continued paid publication.
